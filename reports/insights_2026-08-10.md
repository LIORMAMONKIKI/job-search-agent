### Which Bucket Favours Lior — and Why

**International is the stronger bet, clearly.**

The Israel market is analyst-heavy and SQL-gated — roughly 250 of 871 roles sit behind a BI/SQL wall Lior hasn't cleared yet. The titles that do fit (Technical Artist, Prompt Engineer, AI PM) exist in single digits. Local advantage matters, but the ceiling is low and the competition for those 9–30 matching roles will include candidates with more conventional credentials.

Internationally, Lior's actual stack — ComfyUI, ElevenLabs, visual direction, prompt engineering, model evaluation, data-driven storytelling — is named explicitly in hundreds of JDs, not just implied. The Brand-dominant hiring signal means a strong public portfolio outweighs a traditional resume. The AI Trainer / Generative AI Associate cluster (54+ roles) is a viable income-generating entry wedge that doesn't require SQL. Creative Technologist and Creative Strategist roles are small but clean fits with almost no candidate supply who matches the combination of visual + AI + analytical framing.

The 39 High + 191 Medium international matches versus 14 High locally says it plainly: **Lior has 16× more high-match surface area internationally.**
