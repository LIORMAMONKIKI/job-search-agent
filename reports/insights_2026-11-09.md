### Which Bucket Favours Lior — and Why

**International is the stronger structural fit; Israel is the lower-risk near-term play.**

International wins on:
- **Title match** — Creative Technologist and Generative AI Associate are explicit job categories, not retrofitted descriptions. Lior doesn't have to convince an employer his skills fit; the role was written for his archetype.
- **Skill centrality** — Prompt engineering and ComfyUI appear at scale (403, 108) rather than as niche signals. Lior's core stack stops being a minority credential and becomes table stakes.
- **Absolute volume** — 2.4× more roles means more shots on goal even at the same match rate.

Israel wins on:
- **Zero visa/sponsorship risk** — every role is accessible without an extra approval layer.
- **Network leverage** — local referrals, in-person demos, and community presence compound faster.
- **Speed** — Israel's "AI" roles at early-stage companies (13 vague-title roles) are actively deciding what the job looks like. Lior can shape the definition.

**Bottom line:** Israel is where Lior gets his first role and builds proof-of-work. International is where that proof-of-work cashes out at higher title clarity and likely higher comp. Treat them as sequential, not competing.
